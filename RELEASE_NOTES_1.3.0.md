## Delight Client 1.3.0 for Windows

The launcher opens to your selected instance, with Play and the instance switcher together. Recently played instances appear in the left rail. Browse mods, resource packs, shaders, and worlds from the instance sidebar.

Screenshots now have date groups and side-by-side comparison. Portal brings servers and worlds together. Locker opens on your equipped look, Social opens on your messages, and Alula conversations have their own tab. AutoTune has been removed.

An update appears above your profile picture. Open it to review the release and choose when to install and restart.

On first launch after upgrading, appearance resets to the new defaults. Your instances, game files, accounts, skins, and capes continue to use their existing storage. You can customize Appearance again in Settings.
