## New Features
- Group instances into folders with expandable icon stacks. Drag instances into or out of folders, rename groups, and ungroup without deleting instances.
- Browse the free cosmetics Shop, discover featured capes, and add favorites to your personal Locker before equipping them.
- Redeem one-time cape codes directly in the client.
- Use the in-game emote wheel with Wave, Star Jump, Smooth Shuffle, and Comet Run on supported Fabric instances. Hold B, choose an emote, and release to play.

## Improvements
- Shop and Locker refresh in the background while keeping your collection available during loading.
- Newly claimed capes appear in the correct Locker page and can be equipped separately.
- Updated bundled cosmetics support for Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.2, and 26.3, with version-specific Fabric Loader checks.
- Improved authored emote playback timing and traversal-loop handling.

## Fixes
- Keep cape caches separate for each account and show clear errors when claims or redemptions fail.
- Prevent duplicate redemption submissions and avoid reporting offline claims as successful.
- Handle account changes and overlapping cape refresh/equip requests more reliably.

Multiplayer emote visibility depends on relay availability and compatible clients; it is not guaranteed by this release.
