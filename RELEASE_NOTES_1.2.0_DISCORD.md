# Delight Client 1.2.0 is here!

Bloom Client is now **Delight Client**. An unrelated hacked Minecraft client uses the Bloom Client name, and we want to avoid confusion or any suggestion of affiliation. Delight is the same launcher under a new name, with a new wing logo.

**What's new**

- **Portal** puts your worlds and servers in one place. They're grouped separately in All, and you can search, filter, favorite, or launch them from the list.
- **Launcher controls** have a softer ceramic finish and gentler hover feedback across instances, mods, screenshots, and Settings.
- **Account menu** has a cleaner layout. Click **Log out** and you'll have five seconds to undo it before the client signs you out.

**Windows only.** Open your existing launcher and go to **Settings → Updates**, or download the [Windows installer](https://github.com/Parksdotjar/BloomClient-V2/releases/tag/v1.2.0). Your existing accounts and instances carry over.

This starts the Delight 1.2 series. The next update will be 1.2.1.
