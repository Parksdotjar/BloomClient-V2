# :BloomPetals: **Bloom Client — Patch Notes** :BloomPetals:

## **Version 1.1.9**

### **New Features**

* **Saved appearances:** save your theme, accent colors, background, and surface settings as named presets, then switch between them.
* **Wallpaper colors:** choose accent colors sampled from your current background directly in the color picker.
* **Copy between instances:** drag mods, resource packs, and shader packs to another instance. Bloom checks compatibility, asks before copying unverified content, and preserves existing files.

### **Interface & Animation Improvements**

* Redesigned the **Mods, Resource Packs, and Shaders toolbars** with connected filters, useful installed/enabled counts, and smoother transitions.
* Extended the connected toolbar behavior to the instance library and other main workspaces.
* Simplified **screenshot comparison** into one button that tracks your selection from **0/2 to 2/2**. Hover or focus it before selecting two screenshots to cancel.
* Refined dropdowns, spacing, and motion while respecting reduced-motion and performance settings.

### **Fixes**

* Fixed Equip and Unequip controls briefly appearing over cosmetic names when opening the Locker.
* Preserved cloak and skin selections, equipped indicators, and edited skin models during refresh.
* Improved Locker card sizing, preview layout, and pagination when the catalog changes.
* Fixed keyboard focus restoration when closing screenshot comparison.


*Version 1.1.9 focuses on smoother controls, reusable appearances, and more dependable everyday interactions.*
